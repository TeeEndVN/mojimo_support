Delete your Mojimo account and data

Mojimo

Delete your account in the app

  1. Open Mojimo and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab and tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm twice. If you signed in more than a few minutes ago, the app asks you to sign out and sign in again first. Your account is deleted right away and you are signed out.

Ask us by email

If you no longer have the app, you can ask us instead:

  1. Email support@teeendvn.com with the subject "Delete my Mojimo account".
  2. Send it from the email address of your Mojimo account (your Google account email if you sign in with Google), so we can confirm the account is yours.
  3. We delete your account within 30 days and email you when it is done.

Deleting your account does not cancel a Pro subscription. Cancel it first in Google Play or the App Store.

What is deleted

  • Your account (email address, name, profile picture, Google account identifier or, for email accounts, your hashed password).
  • Your cloud save: saved words with their source sentences, notes and tags, your lists and the words in them, and your device type.
  • Your AI analysis usage records.
  • Any AI results you reported.
  • Pro unlocked with a gift code, and your gift-code redemption records.
  • Your customer record at our subscription management service.

What may be kept

  • Purchase records held by the app stores, kept as required for tax and accounting law.
  • Analytics data and crash reports. They are no longer linked to an active account and are deleted automatically after 14 months (analytics) and 90 days (crash reports).
  • Copies in our database backups, which are overwritten as backups rotate.
  • Emails you sent to support, deleted within 12 months.
  • Anonymous, aggregated statistics that no longer identify you.

Data on your device

Your library and scan history on your phone are not on our servers unless you used cloud save. Delete scan history in Settings, or uninstall the app to remove all local data.
