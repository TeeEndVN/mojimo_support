How to request deletion

  1. Email nghiaht275.work@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Mojimo account".
  2. Send it from the Google account email you use to sign in to Mojimo, so we can confirm the account is yours.
  3. We delete your account within 30 days and email you when it is done.

Deleting your account does not cancel a Pro subscription. Cancel it first in Google Play or the App Store.

What is deleted

  • Your account (email, name, Google account identifier, profile picture).
  • Your cloud save: saved words, lists, notes and device name.
  • Your AI analysis usage records.
  • Analytics and crash data linked to your account ID.

What may be kept

  • Purchase records held by the app stores and our subscription management service, kept as required for tax and accounting law.
  • Anonymous, aggregated statistics that no longer identify you.

Data on your device

Your library and scan history on your phone are not on our servers unless you used cloud save. Delete scan history in Settings, or uninstall the app to remove all local data.
