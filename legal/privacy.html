Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 13, 2026

This policy explains what information the Mojimo mobile app ("Mojimo", "the app") collects, why, and what choices you have. "We" and "us" refer to the developer of Mojimo. If you have questions, email support@teeendvn.com.

1. Summary

  • Screen scans and imported images are processed on your device. Screenshots are not uploaded and are deleted after the text is read.
  • Your saved words, lists and scan history are stored on your device. If you have Pro and are signed in, your library is copied to our servers automatically so you can restore it on another device.
  • If you use Pro AI sentence analysis, the selected text is sent to our server and to an AI provider to produce the explanation. If you report an AI result, the report is sent to us for review.
  • We collect usage analytics and crash reports to improve the app and fix bugs.
  • We do not sell your personal information and we do not show ads. Our analytics service collects your device's advertising ID for analytics only.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Account information

You can sign in with Google, or create an account with your email address and a password.

  • Google: we receive your Google account email address, name, profile picture (if any) and a Google account identifier.
  • Email and password: we store your email address, the name you enter and your password, which is never stored in readable form. To confirm your email and to reset a forgotten password, we email you a one-time 6-digit code.

We use this information to create and identify your Mojimo account, link your Pro subscription and keep your cloud save. You can change your name, and the password of an email account, in the app (Profile → Edit profile).

2.2 Screen captures and scanned text

On Android, Mojimo shows a floating scan button over other apps. When you tap it, the app takes a single screen capture with your permission (Android's screen-capture prompt). The capture is processed on your device by on-device text recognition. It is written briefly to the app's private cache, deleted after the text is recognised, and never uploaded. Small cropped previews of the text areas stay in memory only while the results are open. Mojimo never records your screen continuously.

You can also pick an image from your gallery to scan. It is processed on your device in the same way and is not uploaded.

The recognised text is kept in your scan history on your device. You can turn scan history off and delete all past scans in Settings.

2.3 Your library

Words you save, your vocabulary lists and study data are stored in a database on your device.

If you have Pro and are signed in, cloud save starts automatically. A copy of your library (saved words with their source sentence, notes and tags; your lists and the words in them), the number of words and lists, and your device type (Android or iOS) are uploaded to our database so you can restore or sync them on another device.

2.4 AI sentence analysis (Pro)

When you ask Mojimo to analyse a sentence, the sentence text and your chosen translation language are sent to our server. The server forwards the text to our AI provider (Google Gemini API) to generate the translation and explanation, and returns the result to you. We record how many analyses your account has used this month to apply the monthly limit. Do not analyse text that contains sensitive personal information.

Below each AI result there is a notice with a Report link. If you report a result, we receive the sentence text, the AI's translation and explanation, your translation language, the reason you chose and your account ID, so we can review the result and improve the feature. Reports are not sent to the AI provider.

2.5 Purchases

Pro subscriptions are bought through Google Play or the Apple App Store. We do not receive your card or payment details. A subscription management service receives your purchase receipts, an app user ID linked to your Mojimo account, and basic device information, so we can tell whether your account has Pro.

If you redeem a gift code, we record your account ID, the code you redeemed, when you redeemed it and when the gift Pro ends. To stop guessing of codes, we also briefly record failed redemption attempts linked to your account ID and delete them after 7 days.

2.6 Usage analytics and crash reports

We use an analytics service to record app events such as screens viewed, sign-in, scans (number of results), words saved, AI analyses (result and language), AI result reports (reason and whether they were sent), paywall views, purchases and restores, library limit reached, review prompts, words shared, and Anki exports. We use a crash-reporting service to collect crash reports and error details (device model, OS version, app version, stack traces). When you are signed in, these are linked to your account ID and Pro status. These services also collect an app instance ID and device information. On Android, the analytics service also collects your device's advertising ID; we use it only for analytics, never for ads. You can reset or delete it in Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).

2.7 Data that stays on your device

Dictionary look-ups, Japanese parsing, text-to-speech (using your device's speech engine) and Anki/CSV exports happen on your device. When you share or export a file, it goes only where you choose to send it.

3. How we use information

  • To provide the app's features: sign-in, cloud save, AI analysis and Pro.
  • To check subscriptions and apply usage limits.
  • To understand which features are used and improve the app.
  • To review reported AI results and prevent harmful or wrong output.
  • To find and fix crashes and bugs.
  • To respond to your requests and keep the service secure.

Where data-protection laws such as the GDPR apply, we rely on performing our contract with you (account, cloud save, AI analysis, Pro), our legitimate interests (analytics, crash reporting, security), and your consent where the law requires it.

5. How long we keep data

  • Account and cloud save data: until you delete your account.
  • AI analysis text: not stored by us after the result is returned, apart from usage counts. The AI provider's retention follows its own API policy.
  • AI result reports: kept for up to 12 months, then deleted automatically; deleted sooner if you delete your account.
  • Failed gift-code attempts: deleted after 7 days.
  • Emails you send to support: kept as long as needed to answer you, then deleted within 12 months.
  • Analytics data: kept for up to 14 months.
  • Crash reports: kept for up to 90 days.
  • Data on your device: until you delete it or uninstall the app.

6. Your choices and rights

  • You can use the dictionary and scanner without signing in.
  • You can delete your scan history in Settings, and remove saved words in the app.
  • You can sign out at any time, and delete your account in the app (Profile → Delete account).
  • You can ask us to access, correct, export or delete your personal data. See Delete your account and data, or email support@teeendvn.com. We reply within 30 days.
  • Depending on where you live (for example the EU/UK under the GDPR, California under the CCPA, or Vietnam under Decree 13/2023/ND-CP), you may also have the right to object to or restrict processing, withdraw consent, and complain to your data-protection authority.

7. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Sign-in data is kept in the app's private storage on your device and is excluded from device backups. Your cloud data is protected so that only your account can read it; other users can't read your cloud data. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

8. Children

Mojimo is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you think a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We will change the effective date above and, for significant changes, tell you by email or in the app before they take effect.
