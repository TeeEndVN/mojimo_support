# CLAUDE.md

## Writing style
- Never use em dashes (U+2014) or en dashes (U+2013), including HTML entities `&mdash;`, `&ndash;`, `&#8212;`, `&#8211;`.
- Always use the regular ASCII hyphen-minus `-` (U+002D) instead.
- This applies to all files: HTML, Markdown, legal pages, meta tags, commit messages.
