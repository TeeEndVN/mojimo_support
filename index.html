日本語を、どこでも

Read Japanese anywhere on your phone

Scan text in any app, game or photo to see readings and meanings right away, save new words to your library, and learn the kanji behind them.

Free to download. Scanning and word look-ups are free.

Everything you need to read

Mojimo sits on top of the apps you already use, so you can read without switching away.

⌖

Scan any screen

Tap the floating scan button over any app or game. Text is read on your device and the screenshot is never uploaded.

あ

Tap a word

Sentences are split into words. Tap one to see its reading, meanings and the kanji it is written with.

字

Learn the kanji

Break each kanji down into its parts, readings and meanings, and see other words that use it.

辞

Offline dictionary

Look-ups use a built-in dictionary based on JMdict and KANJIDIC2, so they work without a connection.

★

Build your library

Save the words you find, sort them into lists, and come back to them later.

♪

Hear it spoken

Play any word or sentence aloud with your phone's text-to-speech voice.

Mojimo Pro

Keep every word you find

Scanning and looking up words stay free. Pro is for building your vocabulary, with monthly and yearly plans in the app.

  • Unlimited saved words and vocabulary lists
  • Cloud save for your words and lists
  • Translation and grammar breakdowns, 500 a month
  • Notes, tags and filters for your saved words
  • Export your words to Anki
  • Support an independent app
